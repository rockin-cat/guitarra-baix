Sons de la carpeta "sons/"
==========================
- bateria: SM Drums ("Deeply Sampled Free Drums"), de Real Music Media, domini públic
  (https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SMDrums). Mostres triades (3 capes de força × 2 variants per peça),
  convertides a mp3 mono i normalitzades.
- baix: Swagbass, de Karoryfer Lecolds (D. Smolken), CC0 1.0 (domini públic)
  (https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.swagbass). Capes p, f (2 variants) i fff, mp3 mono normalitzats.
- rhodes, piano: soundfont FluidR3_GM, via https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts (MIT, Benjamin Gleitzman)
- guitarra, guitarra_dist, acustica: soundfont MusyngKite, via https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts (MIT)
