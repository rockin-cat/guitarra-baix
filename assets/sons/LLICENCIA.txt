Sons de la carpeta "sons/"
==========================
- bateria: SM Drums ("Deeply Sampled Free Drums"), de Real Music Media, domini públic
  (https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SMDrums). Mostres triades (3 capes de força × 2 variants per peça),
  convertides a mp3 mono i normalitzades.
- baix: Swagbass, de Karoryfer Lecolds (D. Smolken), CC0 1.0 (domini públic)
  (https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.swagbass). Capes p, f (2 variants) i fff, mp3 mono normalitzats.
- rhodes: jRhodes3c (Rhodes Mark I Stage 73 de 1977), de Jeff Learman, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — ús NO comercial, cal citar l'autor
  (https://github.com/sfzinstruments/jlearman.jRhodes3c). Capes p, f i fff, bucle allargat a 5 s, mp3 mono normalitzats.
  Les guitarres també porten un filtre passa-baixos a 6 kHz per treure el brunzit agut de les gravacions.
- guitarra: Black And Green Guitars (Gretsch Anniversary verda, so «twang»), de Karoryfer Lecolds (Brian Wood), CC0 1.0
  (https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-green-guitars). Una nota cada 3 semitons de Mi2 a Do#6,
  capes p, f (2 variants) i fff, mp3 mono normalitzats.
- guitarra_dist, acustica: soundfont MusyngKite, via https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts (MIT)
